How does bidding work?

Blind Bid: everyone secretly locks a bid, then all bids are revealed. Tied highest bids are resolved by a rotating fair tie-break order.

Open Auction: bids are visible. On your turn, bid any higher whole-dollar amount or pass. The last active bidder wins.
