How Draft Duel works

  1. The host creates a room and shares the code.
  2. Up to eight players join on separate devices.
  3. Every player secretly bids from the same starting budget.
  4. The highest valid bid wins the item and pays that amount.
  5. Tied bids are settled randomly between the tied players.
